# Simulation harness report

Generated by `./gradlew :engine-sim:simReport`. Deterministic: the same code and data give the same report.

**What this shows and does not show.** The harness runs each model against a simulated plant whose behaviour is richer than the engine (hourly demand from sun angle and vapour-pressure deficit scaled by Penman-Monteith ET0, a random plant coefficient, bucket size, pot-wall loss, rain capture and wind sensitivity, uptake that falls as soil dries, rain at a random hour) and a simulated person whose soil-moisture answers are noisy and biased. It shows robustness to plants the engine does not describe exactly. It does not show real-world accuracy: that needs the kitchen-scale measurements.

Weather: 2015-2024 daily history from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (CC BY 4.0; reanalysis from Copernicus C3S). Models see each day's weather and a perfect 7-day forecast; real forecasts are worse.

The baseline uses the same inputs and learning rule as the engine. Its summer interval is the engine's own dry-out time for the plant on the site's mean July day with K = 1, so both start from the same knowledge.

## Metrics

Per plant per year unless stated, averaged over plants.

- **Stress days**: days the true soil got drier than the plant's stress threshold (dry fraction + 0.3, at most 0.98).
- **Over-wet days**: days the true soil stayed near saturation (depletion under 0.05) within 3 days of a watering given while it was still wet.
- **Wet waterings**: waterings given while the true soil was still wet (depletion under 0.3).
- **Prompts**, and the **share answered still wet** ("too wet" or "ok").
- **Dry-out error**: median, over all refills, of the error in the predicted day the plant reaches its dry fraction; in days and relative to the true cycle.
- **Answers to accurate**: median soil-moisture answers (check-ins, plus answers given when watering) before the dry-out prediction is within a day or 20 % for three refills in a row; with the share of plants that never got there. Zero means the starting prediction was already that good.
- **Use error**: mean absolute relative error of predicted daily water use, on days the true plant used at least 20 ml. The real-world version of this metric is against the scale.
